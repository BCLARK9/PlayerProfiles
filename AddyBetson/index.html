Player Summary

Addy is a high-skill player with a 14U-level mastery of most foundational skating, puck handling, shooting, and positioning. Given these strong ability levels, Addy should be continuously challenged in new roles (mentoring new defense teammates), new practices (being open to integrating new pre-game warm-up routines), new game tactics (development of advanced punchturn maneuvers), and enhanced conditioning (participating in speed skating skill competitions). Recommendations for fall season are continued development of shot quickness/accuracy, quick feet acceleration, and stamina to stay maximally engaged in the play deep into shifts and games. Addy is natural leader and role model for her team, and will benefit from adopting a growth mindset and extreme ownership of her own continued development.
