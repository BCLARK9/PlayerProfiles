Player Summary

Mal brings dedication and character to her team. This Spring season has been her first as full-time goaltender for the Revolution. Our focus has been leveraging her natural passion and talents for the position to build up a few technical skills in net. These included reducing glove pop-outs, overslides, and mismanaged depth which have shown progression. Mal's focus and enthusiams will continue to help naturally develop areas like strong-core movements and centered/square positioning in net.

Areas of Strength:

  • Very strong natural puck tracking and reaction time
  • High level of compete and ownership in the goaltending position
  • Solid depth control and butterfly stance
