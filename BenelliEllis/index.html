Player Summary

Benelli has shown large progressions since coming to Rev in 2023. She takes coach instruction and her game play very seriously, which has led to significant development of edgework, foundational wrist shot, and positional fundamentals. This season she has demonstrated new skills as a Center in faceoff leadership, aggressiveness, and wins. Going into fall season, recommendations are for continued development of game-like use of outside edgework, aggressive pressuring with 1-hand and additional strides, and battling both with the puck to escape pressure and without the puck as pass support.
